      How to use your results

      These are ideas to investigate, not promises. Check real local openings, starting pay, program accreditation, total tuition including prerequisites, and whether the schedule is actually predictable. Ask employers about a typical Tuesday—not just their mission statement.

      The matching system is an original, transparent heuristic inspired by established career-interest and work-values frameworks. It has not been independently validated. A high match is not a statistical likelihood of happiness, placement, salary, or job growth.

      Research basis and further verification:
      • O*NET Interest Profiler: six RIASEC interest themes (Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, Conventional).
      • CareerOneStop Work Values Matcher: why values and working conditions matter.
      • BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook: verify requirements, wages and national prospects.
      • My Next Move: review occupational tasks and career preparation.
      Career Compass · Self-contained shareable HTML · For educational exploration only. Occupation examples and training ranges are broad planning approximations, not verified local admissions or job placement statistics.
